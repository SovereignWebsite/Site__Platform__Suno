# Experiments: the Portuguese guitar at the centre

Dinis, 25 Nov 2026: "I really wanted to explore this intersection with fado guitar ... cavaquinho lead with other styles, other instruments, maybe even together with others, but still with the fado guitar being at the epicenter of everything."

Every song in this series: instrumental; the twelve-string Portuguese guitar (guitarra portuguesa) is the only lead and carries a rich, continuous, ornamented melody (no long gaps); the other tradition lives in the rhythm section and the answering instruments; clean, warm, continuous recording; no electric guitar lead, no slap bass, no straight 4/4 rock beat. Learned so far: when a style is named strongly (bossa, funk), Suno tends to drop the guitarra, so say "the Portuguese guitar is the only lead" and put the other style in the rhythm section only. Style influence 0.9, variety 1.
