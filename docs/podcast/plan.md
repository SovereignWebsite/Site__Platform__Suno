## What it is
A radio show of our Suno songs. It starts with the songs Dinis has liked. A presenter introduces each song: where it came from, what was asked, what was tried, what changed between versions. Then the song plays.

## Episode 1: the liked songs
Nine songs Dinis has liked on Suno (read 26 Nov, 12:49 UTC), in the order they were made, so the episode tells the story from Tick Tock (15 Nov) to The Hand-Over Patter (26 Nov). About 26 minutes of music and 7 minutes of presenter. The script is in episode-1-script.md.

## How, with what suno.b already has
- The presenter: Suno's speech tool (speech-create), one clip per introduction plus an opening and a close: 11 clips. Each speech call costs 10 song credits (measured: 110 for the 11 clips) and makes two takes; Suno adds its own voice tag to each take, so the take nearest the opening's voice was chosen.
- The episode, version 1: a public Suno playlist that alternates presenter clips and songs, so it plays straight through in Suno's player, as the newest-10 and newest-20 playlists do. Needs no download credits.
- The episode, version 2 (one audio file, for Spotify): needs the nine songs and eleven clips downloaded and joined. Downloads cost download credits (15 a month), which suno.b spends only at Dinis's word. So version 2 waits for that word, and for b.social's plan for where it goes.

## The presenter's conditions (b.security)
- Labelled synthetic: the opening says the presenter is an AI voice, and each clip's title starts "Presenter (synthetic voice)".
- A stock voice: Suno's own speech voice, no cloned voice, never a real person's voice; no voice_id is sent.
- Every fact in the script comes from site/data/songs.json (the brief, the prompt, the result, the listening tool's notes); nothing invented. No private person is named.
