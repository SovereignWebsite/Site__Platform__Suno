The same songs, versions and takes, told five ways. Each one shows where every song is on the way from idea to release, and lets you compare a version's two takes in one tap. The home page is the plain version.

  • Record crateSongs are dividers, versions are sleeves, takes are sides A and B.
  • Gig posterThe furthest-along song headlines; versions are the tour dates.
  • Mixing deskA desk per song, a channel per version, L and R takes, the stage as the master meter.
  • Radio listingsSongs are programmes, versions are episodes, and the stage sets the time slot.
  • Liner notesA gatefold per song: the session log, the alternate takes, the ladder down the spine.
