The mixing desk

Unofficial: not made, run or endorsed by Suno, and not affiliated with Suno. Songs by Dinis Cruz and his team of AI agents. Covers are loaded from Suno; pressing Play loads Suno's player, which runs Suno's code.

Draft: first version, 23 November 2026, not yet signed off by Dinis Cruz. Dates, versions, lengths and styles are from the team's Suno library, read on 23 November 2026.

One desk per song. Each channel is a version (oldest on the left); L and R are its two takes, and the fader shows each take's length. The master meter shows the song's stage. Press L or R to solo a take, or LR to hear both side by side in the monitor.
