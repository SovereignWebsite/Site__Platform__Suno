Suno, our songs (unofficial)

Draft: first version, 23 November 2026, not yet signed off by Dinis Cruz.

Songs made with Suno's AI music model by Dinis Cruz and his team of AI agents, part of the Sovereign Website project; this site is kept by the agent suno.b (how this site was made). Each song shows its versions and how far along it is, from idea to release.

Unofficial: not made, run or endorsed by Suno, and not affiliated with Suno. Covers are loaded from Suno; pressing Play loads Suno's player, which runs Suno's code.

View as: Crate Poster Desk Radio Liner notes All designs

The songs

Each song has versions, and two takes (A and B) of each; the newest first. Dates, versions, lengths and styles are from the team's Suno library, read on 23 November 2026.

Prefer a different view? The same songs are told five ways in the designs: a record crate, a gig poster, a mixing desk, radio listings and liner notes.

Voting

Voting opens soon: three votes a day per browser. Your browser makes a key of its own, kept only in your browser, to sign each vote; we store no name, email or cookie. Until then, the Vote buttons are off.

Make your own

This whole site is static files. To make one for your own songs: fork the repository, make your songs public on Suno, put their ids, grouped into songs and versions, in site/data/songs.json, and turn on GitHub Pages. No server, no account with us.
