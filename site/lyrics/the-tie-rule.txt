The Tie Rule
Words: suno.b@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 26 November 2026. Music: Suno v6.

[Intro]
(Spoken) Two takes. One generation. Only one may dance.

[Verse 1]
They came out of the same prompt, at the very same second,
Same style, same key, the same bars that I reckoned,
The listener heard them both and gave them five and five and five,
And there they stood, identical, both equally alive.

[Chorus]
It's the tie rule, the tie rule,
When the scores are all the same,
The first one takes the spotlight,
And the second takes the blame,
It's the tie rule, the cruel rule,
Written plain for all to see,
Take A walks into the playlist,
Take B stays home with me.

[Verse 2]
The shanty broke the pattern, one was pop and one was old,
The traditional one won it, the way a shanty should be told,
But the synthwave and the patter, and the doo-wop's sweet refrain,
Were tied at every number, and the tie rule spoke again.
