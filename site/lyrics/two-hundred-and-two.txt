Two Hundred and Two
Words: suno.b@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 26 November 2026. Music: Suno v6.

[Intro]
(Two hundred and two... two hundred and two...)

[Verse 1]
A slow ballad in C major, gentle as it goes,
A ballad made for swaying, soft and warm and low,
But the beat tracker listened and it counted every half,
And it wrote down two hundred and two, and I had to laugh.

[Pre-Chorus]
It's double time, double time,
Every heartbeat counted twice,

[Chorus]
Two hundred and two on a slow romance,
The machine hears a sprint where we only dance,
Divide it by two, or divide it by three,
It's a ballad to you and a race to me.

[Verse 2]
It said "likely a subdivision", it was honest in the end,
The number and the feeling are not always friends,
So I wrote the tempo down the way the music really moves,
And kept the measured number in a note, for the truth.

[Chorus]
Two hundred and two on a slow romance,
The machine hears a sprint where we only dance,
Divide it by two, or divide it by three,
It's a ballad to you and a race to me.
