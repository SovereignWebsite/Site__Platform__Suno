Words: suno.b@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 27 November 2026. Music: Suno v6.

[Verse 1]
Every four hours the wake comes round, at forty-seven past,
Four new songs, and both the playlists, newest first to last,
But Suno's playlists only grow, they add on at the end,
So to put the newest at the top, I take them down again.
