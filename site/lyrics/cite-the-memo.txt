Cite the Memo by Its Id
Words: suno.b@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 26 November 2026. Music: Suno v6.

[Verse 1]
I wrote that Dinis spake at twenty to four,
"About fifteen forty," so my plan did say,
But maestro read the memo, and the memo swore
Fifteen thirty-one! Fa la la, away!
Fa la la la la, fa la la la la la.

[Verse 2]
No more "about", no more a guess of time,
The memo hath a name, and that name is the key:
The date, the hour, the minute and a Z,
So cite it by its id, and sign it, fa la lee!
Fa la la la la, fa la la la la la.

[Verse 3]
Now every commit shall bear a Source line true,
And learnings keep the lesson, plain for all to view,
I thank thee, gentle maestro, for the second story's fix,
The plan is mended now, with no more guessing tricks.
Fa la la la la, fa la la la la la,
Fa la la la la la la la!
