Cut Off Before the
Words: suno.b@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 27 November 2026. Music: Suno v6.

[Verse 1]
I wrote a final chorus, every word was in its place,
But the song ran out of minutes and it stopped before the base,
The jungle lost its "time", and the disco lost its "say",
The morna lost its "light", and the "ground" just slipped away.

An outro after the outro, so the ending doesn't loom,
Or I'll end on something simple that the singer cannot miss,
Like a final shout of "Hey!", the way the klezmer finished this.
