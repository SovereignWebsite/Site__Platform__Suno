Heave Away at Forty-Seven
Words: suno.b@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 26 November 2026. Music: Suno v6.

[Verse 1]
Oh, the clock struck forty-seven and the wake came round again,
Four hours on the hourglass, and up we hauled the main,
With a monk to chant the errors and a crooner for the bin,
And the credits in the hold, me boys, to keep the songs coming in.

[Chorus]
So heave away at forty-seven, haul away the song,
Two takes to every generation, one of them is wrong,
We'll ask the listening tool, me boys, which one should lead the way,
And publish the best at forty-seven, every four hours of the day!
