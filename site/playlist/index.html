Listening board

Every take moves across the board as you listen: from New to Listening, then Liked, OK or Not for me. Not for me is archived out of sight but kept. Like one take of a version and the board offers to archive the other. Drag cards between columns, or open a card's "Move or note".

Suno's player cannot tell this page when a song ends, so press Next. A real mix (fades, layering) needs the audio files, which cost download credits, so it waits. Your board is kept in this browser only until the feedback lane is on.
